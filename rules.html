Entering results

  • Either side can enter the score from the Fixtures page after the match. Sign in with Connect first.
  • Enter the games in each set. When a set finishes 7-6, the tiebreak points are optional.
  • You can correct your result for 24 hours after it is first entered. After that, ask an approver or an admin.
  • Results entered by players show as pending until an approver or admin approves them. Pending results still count.
